Amendr is a done-for-you lead response service.

Amendr was founded in 2025 to solve one problem: service businesses lose jobs because they can't respond to leads fast enough. The average business takes 47 hours to answer a new lead. 78% of customers hire whoever responds first. We make sure that's always our clients.

What we do

Amendr operates the middle of the sales funnel for service businesses. When a lead comes in from any source, our system responds in under 60 seconds, qualifies the lead with a short survey, ranks it by urgency, follows up persistently, and books the appointment directly onto our client's calendar. The business owner doesn't log into software or manage any workflows. We run the entire operation and are accountable for the result.

Who we serve

We work with service businesses where the owner is physically on a job when leads come in: HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, and similar trades. If the first business to respond wins the job in your industry, Amendr makes sure that's always you.

How we're different

Most lead tools hand you software and a learning curve. Amendr is a managed service. We build the system, configure it for your business, run it daily, and report on the numbers. Our clients don't operate a dashboard. They show up to more booked jobs.

The team

Amendr was co-founded by Lucas Fugi (Arizona), Lucas Nakamura (Japan), and Elliot Leung (Hong Kong). We're a small, global team obsessed with lead speed and building the most effective response system in the trades.
